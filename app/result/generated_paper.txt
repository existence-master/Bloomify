Question 1 (Marks: 7.5)

* Sub-question 1.1 (Marks: 3.0)  (Taxonomy Level : Understand)
    Explain the operational model of network security.
* Sub-question 1.2 (Marks: 2.0) (Taxonomy Level : Remember)
    Define vulnerability and write three examples.
* Sub-question 1.3 (Marks: 2.5) (Taxonomy Level : Apply)
    Classify the different categories of information security techniques.

Question 2 (Marks: 7.5)

* Sub-question 2.1 (Marks: 2.0)  (Taxonomy Level : Remember)
    Compare symmetric and asymmetric encryption methods.
* Sub-question 2.2 (Marks: 3.5) (Taxonomy Level : Apply)
    Describe the operation of Feistal cipher and explain its role in block ciphers.
* Sub-question 2.3 (Marks: 2.0) (Taxonomy Level : Understand)
    Explain the concept of weak keys in DES and how they can be exploited.

Question 3 (Marks: 7.5)

* Sub-question 3.1 (Marks: 3.0)  (Taxonomy Level : Analyze)
    Discuss the differences between security and privacy.
* Sub-question 3.2 (Marks: 2.5) (Taxonomy Level : Evaluate)
    Evaluate the strengths and weaknesses of AES compared to DES.
* Sub-question 3.3 (Marks: 2.0) (Taxonomy Level : Create)
    Develop a simple transposition cipher and demonstrate its encryption and decryption mechanisms.

Question 4 (Marks: 7.5)

* Sub-question 4.1 (Marks: 2.5)  (Taxonomy Level : Remember)
    Explain the basic terminology in network security, such as firewall, IDS, and VPN.
* Sub-question 4.2 (Marks: 3.0) (Taxonomy Level : Apply)
    Describe the steps involved in developing a security policy for an organization.
* Sub-question 4.3 (Marks: 2.0) (Taxonomy Level : Understand)
    Discuss the importance of regular security audits and their role in improving network security.
